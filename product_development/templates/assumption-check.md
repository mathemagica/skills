# Assumption Check (Template)

A short, plain-language review for the vision owner, sent after discovery and
before or alongside the proposed scope. It can be done on a 20-30 minute call or
answered in writing. Keep it to one or two pages. Use the owner's own words.

---

## What I heard

[Two or three sentences: the user, the problem and what a better outcome looks
like, in the owner's terms.]

## What I assumed

Please mark each one: **Right**, **Not quite** (and correct it) or **Not sure**.

| # | You said or showed | So I assumed | Right / Not quite / Not sure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | "[quote or description]" ([timestamp or screenshot]) | [assumption in plain language] | |
| A2 | | | |

Include only assumptions that change what gets built or tested. Mark which ones
only real users or buyers can answer; the owner can't confirm those alone.

## Words we should agree on

| Term | What I think it means here |
| --- | --- |
| [e.g. "session"] | [plain explanation] |

Include any term the owner might not know, and any of their terms you're
interpreting.

## Left out of the first version, on purpose

- [feature or idea], because [reason; when it might come back]

## In your own words

Please describe, in a few sentences, what someone would be able to do with the
first version and why it matters. (If your description differs from what I wrote
above, we'll talk it through. That's the point of this step.)

---

Record the owner's answers, the date and the scope revision they apply to. An
answer of "looks fine" without their own description is not enough to treat the
scope as understood; follow up before asking for approval.
