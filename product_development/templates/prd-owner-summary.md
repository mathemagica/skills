# PRD Owner Summary (Template)

A one-page, plain-language companion to the PRD, written for the vision owner's
review and approval. It does not replace the PRD; it points to it. Use the
owner's words, no requirement IDs in the body, no technical jargon.

---

**[Product name], first version — summary of PRD [revision], based on scope [revision]**

## What it's for

[One or two sentences: who it helps and what they'll be able to do.]

## What someone can do with it

1. [Step in the main workflow, as the user experiences it]
2. [Next step]
3. [What they end up with]

## What happens when things go wrong

- [e.g. "If you enter a negative duration, it tells you and doesn't save it."]

## What it won't do yet

- [Excluded feature], [why; what would bring it back]

## What's real and what's simulated

- [e.g. "Uses made-up client names, not your real client list."]
- [e.g. "Sending email is simulated; nothing is actually sent."]

## Decisions we need from you

- [Open decision in plain language, and what it affects]

## Approval

"I approve PRD [revision] as described here and in the full document."

invite users. Those are asked for separately.
