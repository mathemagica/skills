## 2. Users, buyers and operators

Who uses it, who pays, who sets it up. Note where these differ.

## 3. Uncertainties, ranked

| ID | Uncertainty | Type (problem / usability / value / adoption / buying / channel) | Why it could change the decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Include a competitive question when alternatives exist, and a time-to-value or
outcome measure when the product claims to save time or money.

## 4. Experiments, in order

For each: purpose, method, cohort (target and minimum), what is observed versus
what participants report, dates, owner of each task. Start with checks the team
can run without outside participants.

## 5. Participants and recruitment

Qualifying criteria, quotas, channels (each needs authorization before contact),
relationship to the owners recorded per participant, and incentives (if any,
with how they may bias buying signals).

with a reason, and never apply it retroactively.
