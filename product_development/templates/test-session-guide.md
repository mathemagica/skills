# Test Session Guide and Observation Sheet (Template)

For a moderated session where a participant tries the artifact on a realistic
task. The moderator gives tasks, not instructions, and records what happens.

## Before the session

- Confirm consent to observe and, separately, to record; agree where notes go.
- Confirm what the participant will use (their own material stays on their
  device unless agreed otherwise; use synthetic data where real data is sensitive).
- Disclose what is simulated or unfinished.
- Prefer a moderator who did not build the product and is not a close friend
  of the participant; builders and friends tend to soften feedback.

## Opening (5 minutes)

"We're testing the product, not you. Please think aloud. I may not answer
questions right away, because we want to see where it's unclear."

Ask about their most recent real instance of the task: what triggered it, how
they did it, how long it took (measured if they timed it, otherwise estimated).

Help only when the participant is stuck for a set time (e.g. 3 minutes) or asks
to stop. Log every assist.

## Closing questions (non-leading)

- "What did you expect to happen at [moment]?"
- "How does this compare with how you do it now?"
- "What would stop you from using this on your next real [task]?"

Avoid "Do you like it?" and avoid pitching or pricing unless the plan calls for it.
