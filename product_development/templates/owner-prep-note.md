# Owner Prep Note (Template)

Draft this for the facilitator to send before a discovery session. Replace
bracketed text, check that commitments match actual agreements, and keep it plain.
The agent must not send it without specific authorization.

---

Hi [name],

Thanks for agreeing to walk me through your idea for [idea in their words]. Here
is what to expect.

**What we'll do.** We'll talk for about [30-45] minutes over a video call. I'll
ask you to show me things on your screen rather than describe them.

**What to have ready, if you have it:**

- Anything you've already built or sketched, even if it's rough
- The tools or spreadsheets you use today, especially where they fall short
- Diagrams, photos of paper notes or example documents

You don't need any technical knowledge or a finished plan.

**Recording.** With your permission, I'll record the call (audio and screen) and
use [AI tool or service] to transcribe and analyze it. The recording and notes
will be stored [where] and shared only with [who]. Please don't show passwords,
other people's personal information or anything you're not allowed to share.
Tell me if you'd rather I not record; we can use notes or a walkthrough instead.

**Ownership.** [Summarize the actual agreement about the materials you bring,
notes, requirements and any prototype. If not agreed, say what needs clarifying
before work begins; don't make an ownership promise on someone else's behalf.]

**What happens next.**

1. I'll send you a short list of what I understood and what I assumed, for you
   to confirm or correct.
2. Then a plain-language description of the first version, for your approval.
3. Then a requirements document to approve before anything is built.

Nothing gets built, published or shared without your OK at each step.

[Your name]
