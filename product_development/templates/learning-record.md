# Learning Record (Template)

Filled in during and after a market test. Keep it marked **pending** until real
observations exist; never fill it with hypothetical results.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Test plan version | |
| Status | pending / in progress / complete |
| Observation period | |

## 1. Criteria (copied from the approved plan)

| ID | Uncertainty | Success | Failure | Inconclusive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

### Criteria change log

| Date | Change | Reason | Approved by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## 2. Participants (de-identified)

| Code | Role / segment | Channel | Relationship to owners | Incentive | Sessions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## 3. Observations

| Date | Participant | Experiment | Observed behavior | Statement | Measurement | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Keep observed behavior, statements, measurements and inference in separate
columns. Use the workflow contract's labels (stated, demonstrated, inferred,
unknown) where a finding is summarized.

## 4. Buying evidence ladder

| Participant | No interest | Stated interest | Price accepted | Written commitment or pilot | Payment | Repeat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Never add levels together. Record how many people received an offer.

## 5. Criterion outcomes

| ID | Met / not met / inconclusive | Evidence with denominators | Missing data | Alternative explanations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## 6. Recommendation and decision

Recommendation (one of the owner's decision options), with the reasoning and the
next assumption to test. Then the owners' actual decision, date and who decided.
A recommendation is not a business commitment.
